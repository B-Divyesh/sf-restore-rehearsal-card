Privacy, in plain terms.

The CLI is local software. It has no telemetry, analytics, account, or network client.

What stays on your machine

Your manifest, backup paths, checksums, signing keys, results, and Markdown cards stay where you run the CLI. We cannot see or recover them. Cards intentionally omit restored records and command output.

License verification

If you buy or restore the Operator Pack, this site stores the license token and a time-stamped verification result in your browser’s local storage. It sends the token to the Sociobot verification API no more than once per day. The free site and CLI do not require this request.

Checkout

The buy link opens checkout operated by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. Their systems process payment and receipt details under their own privacy terms. This site does not receive card details.

Network and deletion

No analytics or advertising trackers are loaded. To delete local license data, clear site data for this domain. For a purchase support request, use the contact on your Sociobot receipt.
